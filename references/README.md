# Published comparison tables

Associated publication:
Wallis et al. (2025). Canine genome-wide association study identifies
DENND1B as an obesity gene in dogs and humans.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.ads2145

## Source
These workbook copies were supplied by the project owner.
Their original download URLs and download dates have not been recorded.
Checksums identify these supplied copies; they do not independently
authenticate them against publisher-hosted files.

## Files
- s9.xlsx: candidate-gene detection percentages in mouse and human
  hypothalamic data, including cell-type and receptor-positive subgroups.
- s10.xlsx: human cluster-level detection, average-expression and
  co-detection summaries.

## Initial comparison scope
Human DENND1B and MC4R detection metrics only.
Published detection thresholds and subgroup definitions require
verification. S10 cluster mapping also requires verification.
Average-expression comparisons are deferred.

## Handling
Preserve the original workbooks unchanged.
Keep derived comparison outputs separate.
The atlas dataset licence must not be assumed to cover these workbooks.
